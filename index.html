About

Where maritime meets cyber

I sit at the intersection of maritime operations, cybersecurity, and applied research — a space very few people occupy.

At Jetense Consultants Limited, I lead the digital transformation of a maritime consultancy fleet operating AHTS vessels in Nigeria. My work spans from deploying vessel-level network infrastructure and CCTV systems, to authoring enterprise IT security policies, to building Python tools that validate budget workbook chains.

I'm also an MSc candidate at MOUAU, using R and Python for genomics research on Carica papaya — bridging agriculture and data science.

I believe the Nigerian maritime industry is years behind on cybersecurity. I'm working to change that — one policy, one deployment, one training session at a time.
